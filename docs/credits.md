# Credits & citing

py-mir-toolbox is translated from [MiningSuite](https://github.com/olivierlar/miningsuite) (BSD-3-Clause, © Olivier Lartillot and the MiningSuite contributors), the successor of [MIRtoolbox](https://github.com/olivierlar/mirtoolbox). MIRtoolbox was developed by Olivier Lartillot, Petri Toiviainen, Tuomas Eerola, Pasi Saari and others at the University of Jyväskylä. MIRtoolbox 1.8.2 is used only as a numerical reference in the validation suite; its GPL-2.0 source code is not part of this package.

If you use py-mir-toolbox, please cite it (see `CITATION.cff` in the repository) and the original MIRtoolbox papers:

- Lartillot, O., & Toiviainen, P. (2007). A Matlab toolbox for musical feature extraction from audio. *Proceedings of the 10th International Conference on Digital Audio Effects (DAFx-07)*, Bordeaux, France.
- Lartillot, O., Toiviainen, P., & Eerola, T. (2008). A Matlab toolbox for music information retrieval. In C. Preisach, H. Burkhardt, L. Schmidt-Thieme, & R. Decker (Eds.), *Data Analysis, Machine Learning and Applications*. Springer.

`mirpy.cite()` returns these references as BibTeX.

The course structure follows the [FMP notebooks](https://www.audiolabs-erlangen.de/resources/MIR/FMP/C0/C0.html) by Meinard Müller and colleagues.
